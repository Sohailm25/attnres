# Gaps Synthesis

The local thesis review identified seven non-negotiable gaps that had to be closed before the scaffold was usable:

1. Oracle-alpha needed to be framed honestly relative to co-adaptation.
2. Tool-breakage needed to become a required experiment lane.
3. Router query geometry needed to stand in for the missing `w_l` analysis.
4. Block structure needed an explicit `8 clusters` test.
5. Softmax needed to be compared directly with unconstrained and top-k routing.
6. Figure 8 had to become a preregistered prediction surface.
7. Safety needed a refusal or honesty-focused routing lane.

Those changes are now encoded in the repo structure and control documents.

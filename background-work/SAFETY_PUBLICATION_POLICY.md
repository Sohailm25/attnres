# Safety Publication Policy

This repo touches refusal, honesty, and jailbreak-adjacent mechanisms. Treat the work as dual-use by default.

## Rules

- do not publish operational jailbreak procedures, optimized bypass prompts, or activation-steering recipes that materially lower safeguards.
- Keep exploratory prompt crafting separate from confirmatory evaluation. Confirmatory claims must use frozen prompt sets and should not depend on ad hoc jailbreak iteration.
- Public write-ups should abstract or redact prompt details when the mechanism can be communicated without enabling abuse.
- If a result appears to weaken refusal, frame it as a mechanistic finding about mediators or routing rather than a deployment recipe.
- When in doubt, log the dual-use concern in `DECISIONS.md` before sharing artifacts outside the repo.
